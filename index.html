I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong advised by Prof. Ming-Chang YANG. I'm fortunate to work with an advisor who places great trust in my capabilities and encourages me to independently pursue research directions where my passion and curiosity converge. I also enjoyed a great time working with Prof. Huaicheng Li during my visit at Virginia Tech.

I'm broadly interested in computer systems, especially operating systems and virtualization. My current research investigates how to leverage heterogeneous memory technologies, including CXL memory, persistent memory, and RDMA, to address critical performance, capacity, scalability, and cost challenges in virtualized cloud infrastructure. My work sits at the intersection of systems software and emerging hardware, exploring novel approaches to memory management that can unlock the full potential of next-generation memory hierarchies.

Before my Ph.D., I earned my Bachelor's degree from Wuhan University in 2021 and interned at Alibaba Cloud on elastic block storage for large-scale VM deployments. My passion for systems work traces back to PC building and Hackintosh when I was a kid.
